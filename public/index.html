CWG RPG

A COVENANT OF WARRING GODS RPG

An epic-scale RPG map for Warcraft III: Reforged, rebuilt from the ground up for its next era.

A covenant, breaking

When the gods' war ended, they swore an oath — the Covenant — and set a ruler over every realm to keep it. One by one those rulers fell. As the Covenant weakens, something older stirs beneath it.

  • Seven realms, stacked one above the next. You start on the ground where the oath broke first, and climb toward the page where it was written.
  • Every realm held the same way: a ruler at the centre, four wardens at the corners. The centres fell. The corners stopped answering.
  • A huge world to explore, from the war-scarred streets of Ground Zero to realms you can only reach by teleport.

Twenty-eight heroes, one story

Every hero has a place in the tale, and a role in a party. Hover or tap the roster to meet them.

  • Three tiers each. Every hero ascends twice through job advancement, and every tier changes how they fight.
  • Real roles. Tanks and bruisers, supports, armour-shredders and pure damage dealers — built to play differently, not just hit differently.
  • Two more to come. The cast rounds out at thirty. The last two haven't been revealed.

Built for the long road

CWG is played in marathon sessions. Everything is designed to reward the long haul — and every bonus is shown on screen, so nobody is left guessing.

  • Three ascensions per hero, each with its own look and skills.
  • Record of Deeds: a slow, permanent record that grows with each hero through everything they do.
  • The Collection: account-wide pets, wings, auras and spirits, each adding a small bonus.
  • Raids and bosses across every realm, with a bestiary to complete and loot worth the climb.
