CWG RPG 1.30 enters testing

The Lua rebuild of CWG is far enough along to put in front of players. Here's what testing covers, and what it doesn't.

What 1.30 is

Version 1.30 is the first release of CWG rebuilt in Lua: a cleaner foundation, a new save system, and a lot of quality-of-life work. Why we rebuilt it covers the background.

What testers are doing

A small group of testers is now playing the new build. Their job is to break it (loading, saving, long sessions, big fights) so problems are found before release, not after.

  • Stability first. Long sessions, saving and loading, and the kinds of moments that used to desync games.
  • A full balance pass. Skills should feel weighty again, and casting one should never feel like losing damage.
  • Every system checked. If something gives you a bonus, it should say so on screen.

The test build doesn't save or load, on purpose. Nothing a tester does can touch anyone's real progress.

When is it out?

When it's ready. We'd rather ship it late than ship it broken; this one has to carry the game for a long time. There are also a few things in 1.30 we're not talking about yet.

Updates, tester calls and the release announcement land on the Discord first.
