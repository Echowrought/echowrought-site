Why we rebuilt the whole map in Lua

CWG has been patched hard since July. Version 1.30 rebuilds it in Lua, so every update after it is easier than the ones before.

The map we had

The CWG most of you play today is built on JASS, Warcraft III's original scripting language. It has carried the game a long way, but it has also carried months of patches stacked on patches, done fast and under pressure. Some parts had become hard to read even for us, and every fix risked breaking something two systems over.

For a while we were effectively working on two maps at once: keeping the live JASS map patched while the Lua rebuild took shape beside it. Every fix had to be made twice. That doesn't scale, and it isn't fun.

The problems that kept coming back

Some bugs never really went away, no matter how many times they were patched, most of all around saving and loading, where two players loading at once, or a hit landing mid-load, could desync a game or crash it.

CWG is played in very long sessions. People keep the same game running for days. In a map like that, small leaks and rare crashes add up, and stability stops being a nice-to-have and becomes the whole foundation.

Why Lua

  • A modern language. Real data structures and cleaner code mean each system can live in its own place and be changed without tripping over the others.
  • Tests before players. Changes are checked automatically before they ever reach a lobby.
  • Balance by measurement. We built a combat simulator that runs the map's own code, so hero changes are measured against real bosses instead of guessed at.
  • Finding leaks and slowdowns. We can see what's using memory and frame time during a long game, and fix the worst offenders directly.
  • Community tools. Lua opens the door to libraries and effects made by the wider Warcraft III modding community.

What it means for you

  • One map, so every update reaches everyone at once.
  • A new save system. Saves are rebuilt from scratch, and your existing saves carry over automatically.
  • Steadier games. Loads take turns instead of colliding, and the long-standing desync and crash spots have been worked through one by one.
  • Nothing hidden. Every bonus and mechanic is shown in the interface, so nobody has to guess what's affecting their hero.
It's almost a whole new game under the hood, built so the next update doesn't have to fight the last one.

What happens next

The Lua version ships as CWG RPG 1.30. It's in testing now; here's what that means. Release notes, tester calls and the occasional surprise go to the Discord first.
