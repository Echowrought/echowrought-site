Why we rebuilt the whole map in Lua

For a long time CWG lived two lives at once. Version 1.30 is where it finally becomes one game again.

Two maps, one game

CWG grew up on Hera, an older hosting platform for Warcraft III. When players started moving to Reforged, the map followed, and for a while that meant keeping two separate versions alive side by side: the original on Hera, and the Reforged port most of you play today.

Every fix and every feature had to be made twice. The two communities drifted to different points in the game, and some features only ever existed on one side. Meanwhile Hera has slowly wound down, and most players have already made the move to Reforged.

Why not just patch it?

The old map carries years of patches, built on Warcraft III's original scripting language. It works, but it's hard to change safely. Some problems kept coming back no matter how many times they were patched, most of all around saving and loading, where two players loading at once, or a hit landing mid-load, could desync a game or crash it.

CWG is played in very long sessions. People keep the same game running for days. In a map like that, stability isn't a nice-to-have; it's the whole foundation.

What Lua changes

  • One map. A single Lua codebase on the latest Reforged, so every update reaches everyone at once.
  • Everything from both versions. Features that only ever existed on Hera are being brought across, so nobody loses anything in the merge.
  • A new save system. Saves are rebuilt from scratch, and your existing saves carry over automatically.
  • Nothing hidden. Every bonus and mechanic gets shown in the interface, so nobody has to guess what's affecting their hero.
  • Settings for everyone, not just the host, including reduced effects that actually apply to every effect.
It's almost a whole new game under the hood, built so the next few years of updates don't fight the last few.

What happens next

The Lua version ships as CWG RPG 1.30. It's in testing now; here's what that means. Release notes, tester calls and the occasional surprise go to the Discord first.
