November 2026Patch notesIn testing

CWG RPG 1.30

The Lua update. Three weeks, 793 commits, and one very tired keyboard.

General Heroes Bosses & Raids World Items & Loot New Systems Interface Saves & Stability Bug Fixes

1.30 is in testing. These notes may still change before release.

General

  • The whole map is rebuilt in Lua. Same game, new bones. Why we did it.
  • New save system. Old saves convert once on your first load, then you're on the new one for good.
  • Loads take turns: first come, first served, a few seconds apart. You're told your place in line. No more two people loading at once and taking the lobby down with them.
  • Single player is back. Warcraft cheat codes still work; they just switch off saving for that game. Nice try.
  • All remaining Korean text translated. Big numbers read as K / M / B.
  • Names borrowed from other games and shows are replaced with names from our own world. Your saves and chat commands don't change.
  • New loading screen, character select backdrop, lobby text and map preview.
  • The map file is a good deal smaller.

Heroes

  • Every hero has new art and icons for all three tiers. Character select uses them.
  • When full Mana fires your burst, it now waits 3 s with a light over your hero. Press your signature button to release it at full strength, or leave it and it goes off at half.
  • The shared speed button is gone. Every hero gets their own button instead.
  • Every hero gets one mechanic that's theirs alone (see below).
  • Casting never costs you damage. Stop auto-attacking out of fear.
  • Full balance pass. Heroes were measured against real bosses, then tuned to their role. Some went up, a few came down, nobody got deleted.
  • Every damage number in every skill tooltip checked against the actual code, and they follow your hero's current form.
  • Blinks reach 1000 range.
  • Elements, the Nature element and Potential hit the same on every hero form.
  • Runes turn on just by being carried: hero, bag or storage.

Abyss

  • New: Release on command. Full Mana lights the button; let it go yourself for full strength.
  • Spears now fly to the target instead of wherever they felt like.
  • Rune of Abyss is wired up. It does things now.

Bosses & Raids

New raids

  • Ruinwing and Varthion raids.
  • The Twilight Brood raid, with Nyxareth and company.
  • The Abraham raid. Bring friends. Bring more friends.
  • Hell Expedition, with bosses that scale with each stage.

Raid roles

  • Raid crafts now carry a raid stat: Bulwark on STR, Ward on INT, Precision on AGI. Gloves get it first, then the accessory, then the weapon. Each enhancement level adds +1.
  • Ganesha, Primigenius, Leviathan and every Primordial raid boss now go after the Guardian with the most Bulwark. Guardians: Aran, Frenzy, White King and Nature. (The Record lists one more, but she hasn't answered the summons yet.)
  • Their attacks stack Rend: +20% per hit, up to 10 stacks, gone 6 s after the last one.
  • Bulwark cuts their attack damage by up to 70%: full effect at 30 against the Ancient three, 50 to 95 in the Primordial Realm. Bring a tank. Or a lot of potions.
  • Ward cuts their pattern damage by up to 20%, for you and allies within 400.
  • Precision adds up to 20% damage against them, doubled while they stagger.
  • Aeonvar now calls Sundering Blow and turns on whoever isn't tanking. His Frozen Moment freezes one hero: an ally has 4 s to stand in the green circle for 1 s, or that hero is erased. Bring a friend.
  • Taunts now interrupt a boss mid-cast: the move is cancelled and the boss staggers for 1.5 s. Moves triggered at health thresholds can't be interrupted.
  • Boss moves call themselves out by name over the boss before they land, instead of filling your chat.
  • Your Bulwark, Ward and Precision are on the Character Card.

Every boss fight

  • One system now runs every pattern boss. One pattern at a time, cast from up to 1,500 away, no more walking over to hit you.
  • Ground telegraphs on every attack, with a fill that shows when it lands. Red hurts. Green is safe. Purple kills.
  • Every cast warns first, and the warning text says what actually happens.
  • Raid bosses blink home when dragged out of their zone.
  • Arcadiel, Myrkalis and Aeonvar reset HP and Mana when the raid disengages.

Specific bosses

  • Ruinwing: Ring of Doom wave 2 spares the inner disk.
  • Teneph stands his ground. His Northern Warden now carries a real aura (-30% Movement Speed nearby).
  • Liopleurodon's maelstrom is back to 4 waves.
  • Zarktul's storm shows its safe zone.
  • Somnaria's Life fields 150M. Raging Tide no longer shreds armor.
  • Kronos no longer spawns in the event room. He wasn't invited.
  • Hovering over Basilgar no longer crashes the game.

World & Gameplay

  • Travel menu: 7 realm tabs and new destinations.
  • You can no longer walk, blink or dash across zone borders. Yes, that shortcut. Sorry.
  • Field portals to bosses have a rune ring on the ground and the boss's name above it.
  • The ghost ships' decks are solid ground now.
  • The day/night clock is gone. The sky in these realms hasn't moved since the war ended; the map just stopped pretending.
  • Terra's giants and the Black Brood field spawn when you travel there, not when you wander in.
  • Event zone: Sunfang and Poporing have moved in. Sunfang drops Sunfang's Ward (+2 to +10).

Awakening

  • Awakening promotions now have pity. Every failed promotion kill adds +0.05% to that tier's chance until you promote, then it starts over at the next tier.
  • Your current pity shows on the failure message and in -awakening. It's saved per hero.
  • Godhood used to take about 100 Strak kills on average. Now it's about 41, and it always ends eventually. Strak has been informed. Strak is not thrilled.
  • The Max HP Awakening path no longer stops growing at Godhood. Dawn, Ancient, Genesis, Origin and Apocalypse now give +60M, +65M, +75M, +85M and +100M Max HP.

Enhancement

  • A failed Enhancement Stone on a +5 attempt now leaves behind 1 Enhancement Stone Fragment.
  • Fragments still combine at 3,000 into a Perfect Enhancement Stone, so long dry streaks on the +5 step finally pay a little something back.
  • Only the +5 step drops dust. Your lucky friend gets nothing, and that's the point.
  • Fragments go to your storage, the same as party dungeon fragments.

The Hollow

  • Hollow Night has ended. The Hollowbound and Elites stayed anyway.
  • Slain Colossal and Named bosses have a 2% chance to rise again as Hollow-touched: double health, 1.5x damage, and the Hollow's Mark (a sigil that bursts for 25% max HP; never lethal).
  • Hollow-touched bosses drop one guaranteed item from their own table, plus lumber for everyone nearby, and get their own Bestiary entry.

Items & Loot

Drops

  • Raid loot rolls for everyone in range. New setting: Solo (straight to your bags) or Group (held for 60 s).
  • One roll per item per kill, and the chances match the drop-table panel. The drop-table button lists every drop.
  • Escalating odds: each kill adds +0.5% to items above 1%, shown on every boss from the first kill.
  • Anya never takes an item held for someone else, and auto-pickup works with her again.
  • Rare wings from the Elemental Spirit rare mobs: Hollowfeather, Bloodglass, Starless Veil and Covenant Dawnwings.

Gear & progression

  • Gear tiers 31–50, the Primordial book shop, and Genesis gear (Anakrum, Anakiel, Nephiragon).
  • Runes: 155 of them, with engraving and enhancement.
  • Relics: fragments drop from the new bosses. Transcend them I to V on a brand-new panel.
  • Elemental Resonance goes to +60.
  • Boss Subjugation Chests, and a crafting chain we're not going to spoil.
  • Attack speed can go past +400%.

Tidying up

  • Essences are named after the boss that drops them. No more shared names.
  • Tooltips show stats, break chances and craft chances.
  • Enhancement reads +1, +2, +3 with no gaps, and the success message names the right level.
  • Every item the map can make can be saved.
  • Hollow Lantern stacks stop at level 5 (some of you saw 24).

New Systems

Record of Deeds

  • Earn Deeds in every realm. Higher realms pay a lot more.
  • Spend them to level the Record for main stat. Early levels are cheap.
  • 8 Inscriptions fill by level 1,000, then Endless Will keeps going forever.
  • Lives on its own button on the HUD.

The Collection

  • One painted panel, 8 tabs: wings, auras, pets, bags, Tailed Spirits and more.
  • Bags collect themselves, account-wide. Change Look lets any bag wear a look you've collected.
  • Every entry says where it comes from.
  • Hero effects count as auras: +3% aura damage each. The full set is any 14 of 19.
  • Skins tab is in. Skins are coming in a later update.

Achievements

  • Achievements belong to your account and count back to your first game.
  • 40 monster-family achievements, raid challenges (Deathless, Few Hands), and Tier II / Tier III victories for every raid boss.
  • Achievements for huge roll counts carry a tiny bit of pity. We're not monsters.

Also new

  • Bestiary: 12 new bosses, a milestone every 8%, one realm per page.
  • Character Card: base and bonus stats, elements, every defence source, Familiars page.
  • The Core, Sailing Potential (+1 to +5), an 11th storage bag, companions and riding mounts.
  • Beginner and Otherworld quests.
  • Pet buffs reach their owner anywhere on the map, and every pet effect has a visible button.

Interface

  • Added a DPS meter in-game. On the Training Dummy it starts on your first hit and shows DPS, total, biggest hit and your top skills. On raid bosses it ranks everyone's damage, DPS and share. Settle your arguments.
  • Settings: your own Effects (Full / Reduced) and Screen Shake. Host keeps Performance Mode. Kill-reward text is off unless you want it.
  • Status bar: 13 buff slots, temporary effects first, one slot each for Companions and Tailed Spirits. No more flicker on hover.
  • Panels open from HUD buttons, not chat commands. Esc closes every menu.
  • Monster and boss names sit under the portrait, like heroes'.
  • No more Warcraft dialog boxes.
  • Quest box follows the edge of widescreen monitors.
  • Chat typed during character select waits for your hero.

Saves & Stability

  • Save files are about a third smaller, so they reach everyone faster. -savestatus shows how long yours took.
  • If a hero can't load, the game tells you why.
  • Loading work moved behind the loading screen, so games settle faster after everyone loads.
  • Multiplayer desyncs fixed, including the one from Relic Transcend.
  • Panel clicks only act for the player who clicked.
  • Big memory-leak cleanup. One status board was making 19 new objects per player, per second. It has been spoken to.
  • Crash fixes: command buttons with nothing selected, status icons on hidden units, the day clock, and more.
  • 457 doodads that were invisible are back. Walls close their gaps, and the planets are no longer black.

Bug Fixes

  • On-attack effects inside conditions ran twice per attack. Now once.
  • Companion bag forms no longer double their bonuses. It was nice while it lasted.
  • Peter's Firmament works.
  • Toucan boss damage refreshes by itself.
  • The Black Dragon no longer drops Umbrael.
  • Tooltips with commas show in full.
