November 2026Patch notes

CWG RPG 1.31

The first patch after launch. Thank you for every report.

Heroes Bosses Saves Bug Fixes

Newer: CWG RPG 1.32 patch notes. Same model pack as 1.30. See also the 1.30 patch notes.

Heroes

  • Lightning: new passive, Storm Skin, on every tier. He takes 15% less damage, and every enemy his attacks and bolts hit heals him 0.2% of max HP (up to 0.6% a second). It shows on his status bar.
  • White King: Peter's Firmament lasts the full 10 seconds its tooltip says (it ended after 8). Same total healing.

Bosses

  • Bosses in ticket dungeons and farming areas no longer hunt you across their whole area. They fight whoever comes to them, and still go home if dragged out: Sereina, Karabaz, Storm-Ape King, Varkan, Avalon, Tyrannorex, the Yaksha King, Arcadiel, Inorex, Leviathan Melvillei, Strak, Ganesha and Morkazar.
  • Field monsters that appear when you first arrive respawn again: Metatron X, Charging Juggernaut, Colossus 77, Ground Zero's infested, Kradin, Valgram, Arcanion and the ticket dungeon mobs.

Saves

  • Wearing a title no longer sends your saves to a different file. Anything saved while a title was on is found and restored the first time you start 1.31: the further-along copy of each hero comes back, and your account and Collection are merged. You'll get a message if anything was restored.

Bug Fixes

  • The DPS meter shows players' BattleTags, not their titles.
  • Pet auras (Tigrath, Shuriman, Kain, Lumiel) and the Black Scale Blessing no longer show up as buttons on your hero.
  • A hero stuck in the black space outside the world is brought back to town.
